Roman HLWAS, DES Y6, and Rubin LSST WFD footprints are vector outlines extracted (marching-squares contouring + simplification) from the original HEALPix coverage maps at their native resolution, so boundaries stay smooth at any zoom instead of showing a coarse raster grid. Euclid/HSC outlines are the original digitised vertex loops. Background: NASA/SVS Milky Way mosaic. All data bundled inline — no external requests (background image loads from this same page's folder). Adapted from a matplotlib/healpy tool originally by Steven Benton (Princeton).
