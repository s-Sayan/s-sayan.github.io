I am an observational astrophysicist specializing in weak gravitational lensing and galaxy cluster astrophysics. As a postdoctoral researcher at Northeastern University, I lead the development of the end-to-end, pixel-to-science pipeline for NASA's balloon-borne imaging telescope, [SuperBIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super-pressure_Balloon-borne_Imaging_Telescope). My postdoc advisor is [Prof. Jacqueline McCleary](https://cos.northeastern.edu/people/jacqueline-mccleary/).

Before joining Northeastern, I completed my PhD at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune, in September 2024, under the guidance of [Prof. Tarun Souradeep](https://sites.google.com/view/tarun-souradeep/). My doctoral research primarily focused on developing Bayesian inference techniques for Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) data analysis. During my PhD, I was awarded the [Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship](https://cosmology.unige.ch/users/sayan-saha) (2022–23) which allowed me to work with [Prof. Julien Carron](https://cosmology.unige.ch/users/julien-carron) at the University of Geneva on studying high-redshift galaxy clusters using CMB lensing. My PhD thesis can be found [here](https://shodhganga.inflibnet.ac.in/handle/10603/637309).
