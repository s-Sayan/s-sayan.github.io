I am an observational astrophysicist specializing in weak gravitational lensing and galaxy cluster astrophysics. As a postdoctoral researcher, I have been working on weak-lensing systematics for the first-of-its-kind balloon-borne imaging telescope, [SuperBIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super-pressure_Balloon-borne_Imaging_Telescope). I am currently working with my postdoctoral advisor, [Prof. Jacqueline McCleary](https://cos.northeastern.edu/people/jacqueline-mccleary/).

Before joining Northeastern University, I completed my PhD at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune, under the guidance of [Prof. Tarun Souradeep](https://sites.google.com/view/tarun-souradeep/), where I mostly worked on studing violations of statistical isotropy in the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) data. During my PhD, I was a [Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship](https://cosmology.unige.ch/users/sayan-saha) (2022–23) at the University of Geneva, working with [Prof. Julien Carron](https://cosmology.unige.ch/users/julien-carron) on maximum a posteriori (MAP) methods for measuring galaxy cluster masses using CMB gravitational lensing. My PhD thesis can be found [here](https://shodhganga.inflibnet.ac.in/handle/10603/637309).
