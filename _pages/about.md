I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Northeastern University, working with [Prof. Jacqueline McCleary](https://cos.northeastern.edu/people/jacqueline-mccleary/) on **SuperBIT**, a NASA balloon-borne imaging telescope. I lead the development of its weak-lensing shape-measurement pipeline, working on PSF characterization and modelling, shear measurement systematics, and neural-network-based shape measurement techniques for weak lensing galaxy surveys.

Before that, I completed my Ph.D. in Physics at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune, under the guidance of [Prof. Tarun Souradeep](https://sites.google.com/view/tarun-souradeep/), with a thesis on novel Bayesian inference methods for the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB). During my Ph.D., I held a [Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship](https://cosmology.unige.ch/users/sayan-saha) (2022&ndash;23) under the guidance of [Prof. Julien Carron](https://cosmology.unige.ch/users/julien-carron) at the University of Geneva, where I developed maximum a posteriori (MAP) methods for measuring galaxy cluster masses from CMB lensing.

My research interests span observational and computational astrophysics, with a particular focus on end-to-end image processing pipelines for weak lensing surveys, galaxy cluster dynamics, and Bayesian inference methods for cosmology and CMB data analysis.

I occasionally write about research and life in academia on my [blog](/blog/). You can find my other profiles &mdash; GitHub, INSPIRE-HEP, LinkedIn, Instagram &mdash; in the icons below.
